## 🚀 Trackify Pro — Universal Business Operating System

Located in the [`app/`](./app/) directory, **Trackify Pro** is a modern, high-performance web application designed for retail shops, pharmacies, kirana stores, and apparel outlets.

### Key Features:
- 📊 **Real-Time Daily P&L Dashboard**: Live sales tracking & net profit margins.
- 🛒 **Fast POS Billing Counter**: Barcode search, item discounts, auto GST 12% calculation.
- 📕 **Customer Udhar Khata Ledger**: Outstanding credit management with WhatsApp payment reminders.
- 💊 **7 Business Category Catalogs**: Kirana, Garments, Electronics, Hardware, Pharmacy, Restaurant, Services.
- 📸 **Camera Barcode Scanner & Counter UPI QR**: Instant payment confirmation modal.
